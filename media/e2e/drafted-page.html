What we already know

Top-quartile organisations process 2.3x the POs per buyer of the median. The gap is almost entirely low-value tail spend that never needed a buyer.

34% of purchase orders raised in Q3 were under EUR 2,000. Median approval chain for those was 3 people and 6.2 days.

Every buyer named the same two: re-keying supplier data from email, and chasing approvals that nobody disputes.

Where does the 10% most plausibly come from? Use only the three sources here.

Tail spend is the only pool big enough. 34% of POs are under EUR 2,000 and carry a three-person approval chain that the audit found nobody ever rejects. Auto-approving that band would take roughly a third of PO volume off the buyers without touching the categories where negotiation earns money. On the interview evidence that is about 12% of buyer time, which clears the target.
