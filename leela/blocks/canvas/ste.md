Write in ASD-STE100 simplified technical English.

- Keep each sentence to 20 words or fewer. A list of three parallel clauses may run longer.
- Use the present tense and the active voice. Name the doer.
- Use one word for one meaning. Never vary a term for texture.
- Use the plainest word that is still correct.
- Write the article: "the reader reads the file", not "reader reads file".
- Do not use an em dash, a semicolon, or a parenthetical aside.
- Write sentences, not a list.

Say each thing once. A point restated in other words is padding, and so is a sentence whose only
job is to announce the next one. Cut any sentence that carries no fact of its own. Do not close on
a reversal or on a pair of matching sentences.
